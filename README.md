Project Overview: E-commerce Sales & Profit Analysis

Business Insights & Findings

1. Market Leadership & Sales Distribution:

    Top Performer: The USA is the leading market in terms of sales volume, followed by France and the UK.

    Regional Trends: Customer preferences vary significantly by country. For instance, Fashion is the top-selling category in the USA, while Home Decor dominates the UK market.

    Data Integrity: A noticeable portion of orders comes from 'Unknown' countries, suggesting a need for better customer profile data collection.

2. Category-wise Profitability:

    Highest Profit Margin: Although Fashion has the highest sales percentage (20.1%), the Sports and Beauty categories contribute the most to the actual net profit (~19.9% each).

    Low Performing Category: The 'Others' category shows the weakest performance, contributing only 4.8% to the total profit.

3. Operational Challenges (Order Status):

    High Return Rates: A critical insight is the high number of 'Returned' and 'Cancelled' orders in Beauty, Sports, and Electronics. In several regions, these numbers are nearly equal to successfully 'Delivered' orders.

    Shipping Impact: France has the highest average shipping cost, yet it remains the most profitable country (Net Profit > 14,000), indicating a high-value customer base.

Strategic Recommendations

    Reduce Return Rates: Investigate the root causes of high returns in the Beauty and Sports Sectors (e.g., product quality or sizing issue).
    
Inventory Optimization: Reallocate budget from the 'Others' category to high-margin categories like Sports and Beauty to maximize ROI.

Targeted Marketing: Launch category-specific campaigns based on regional demand (e.g., Fashion-focused ads for the USA and Home Decor for the UK).

Logistics Efficiency: Explore ways to optimize shipping in France to further increase the already high profit margins.
